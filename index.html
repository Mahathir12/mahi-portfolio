01 / ABOUTA LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I’m Mahathir Mohammad Mahi, an Electrical & Computer Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), with a current CGPA of 3.55. I am interested in building practical solutions through programming, electronics, embedded systems and intelligent technologies.

I completed my Secondary School Certificate (SSC) at Jamalpur Zilla School with a GPA of 5.00, followed by my Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) at Dhaka Residential Model College, where I also achieved a GPA of 5.00. I am now pursuing my B.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at RUET.

My technical interests include software development, C/C++ programming, robotics, embedded systems, automation and artificial intelligence. I have worked on student-focused web applications including OOPQuizBot, LifeTracker and PitchCode, alongside hardware projects such as an STM32-based Environmental Monitoring Module prototype and a Self-Balancing Robot.

Leadership and collaboration are also important parts of my experience. I have served in student and community organizations in several roles, including election administration, executive committee responsibilities, community administration and Class Representative of the ECE’23 Series from 2024 to 2026.

Alongside engineering, I enjoy creative and media-related work. I have contributed scriptwriting and direction to RUET Career Forum promotional videos, designed a RUET ECE Club magazine and created graphic design materials. I value teamwork, communication, event coordination and continuous learning, and I am interested in opportunities where I can contribute, develop my skills and work on meaningful projects.
